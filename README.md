## **Assessment**

This part of the module, which runs through Teaching Block 1 (TB1), is listed as Item 1 in the module specification. Your module (PAPL — M30235, or UXDI — M32605, depending on your cohort) has two assessments; Item 1 is worth **50% of the overall module mark**.

Item 1 is assessed as a portfolio of five live in-person demos of your coursework app. You build the app progressively by completing the exercises at the end of each weekly worksheet. Every two worksheets prepare you for one demo (for example, Worksheets 1 and 2 prepare you for Demo 1). You submit the link to your public GitHub repository on Moodle and then demonstrate your work in class during your timetabled practical sessions.

### **Demos**

There are five demos across the term, but only your best four demo marks count towards Item 1. Each counted demo is worth 25% of Item 1 (12.5% of the overall module mark). Each demo is assessed on functionality (9% of Item 1), code quality (8%), and your understanding through two questions about your own code (8%).

For Demos 1 to 3, a zero on any required functionality item caps the final demo mark at 23 out of 25. Lower marks remain unchanged. Demo 3 asks one understanding question from Worksheet 5 and one from Worksheet 6, excluding advanced, optional extensions.

Demo 3 covers navigation, a ticket basket, profile forms, a JSON film catalogue and saved profile preferences. SQLite booking history and database-backed checkout begin in Worksheet 7 for Demo 4, alongside organised tests and demonstrated editor coverage. Provider and notifiers are not required.

Assessment takes place across five two-week demo windows (Weeks 1 and 2, Weeks 3 and 4, Weeks 5 and 6, Weeks 7 and 8, and Weeks 9 and 10). Three rules apply:

- You can perform at most one demo in any demo window.
- Demos must be completed in order. You cannot skip ahead to a later demo if you have missed an earlier one, because each stage builds on the previous codebase.
- Missing one demo window carries no mark penalty because only your best four demo marks count. Missing two or more windows reduces the number of demos you can complete.

⚠️ You will only receive marks if you attend your timetabled practical. You cannot get marks in practicals at a different time to what is shown on your timetable.

For the full detailed explanation of the assessment read the [Assessment Guide](https://portdotacdotuk-my.sharepoint.com/:w:/g/personal/mani_ghahremani_port_ac_uk/IQC9nZoNwb2jT40MnFQPZWVvAVdTK2PGBmZ8jPff30RRyPc).

### **Retrieval of failure**

The retrieval of failure period for Term 1 takes place in the week beginning Monday 22 February 2027 (22/02/2027 to 26/02/2027). If your total mark across all demos before the Christmas break is under 40%, you have not passed Item 1 and must arrange a meeting with me during this period to complete your remaining demos (or all demos if none were completed). You do not need to start a new coursework; you continue and finish the same Southsea Cinema coursework.

### **Referral/deferral assessment**

Your assessment during the retrieval period in February depends on whether you have an approved Extenuating Circumstances Form (ECF):

- **Approved ECF (deferral):** Your mark in February is uncapped.
- **No approved ECF (referral):** Your overall mark for Item 1 is capped at 40%.

### **Extenuating Circumstances**

If external events affect your study for more than one to two weeks (such as physical or mental illness, family issues, problems with accommodation, or laptop and vehicle breakdown), submit an [Extenuating Circumstances Form (ECF)](https://myport.port.ac.uk/my-course/exams/extenuating-circumstances#apply) with supporting evidence as soon as you can.

⚠️ Once your ECF is approved, you must email me ([mani.ghahremani@port.ac.uk](mailto:mani.ghahremani@port.ac.uk)) so your assessment records can be updated.

Please still attend practical sessions and complete as many demos as possible during term time. ECFs apply to the whole of Item 1 (the Flutter Coursework portfolio, 50% of the module) rather than individual demos. For full assessment rules, see the [Assessment Guide](https://portdotacdotuk-my.sharepoint.com/:w:/g/personal/mani_ghahremani_port_ac_uk/IQC9nZoNwb2jT40MnFQPZWVvAVdTK2PGBmZ8jPff30RRyPc).
